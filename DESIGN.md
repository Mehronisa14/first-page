## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the tactile warmth, quiet luxury, and unhurried ritual of an artisanal specialty cafe. It merges refined editorial craftsmanship with modern minimalist utility.

### Brand Personality & Core Atmosphere
- **Intimate & Welcoming:** Radiates warmth and comfort without unnecessary clutter; feels like stepped-in morning sunlight on clean timber.
- **Artisanal & Mindful:** Celebrates intentional detail, craftsmanship, and slow living.
- **Tactile & Elegant:** Balances literary, grounded serif typography with generous negative space and velvet-soft surfaces.

### Design Movement
**Warm Editorial Minimalism with Soft Tactile Nuance.** The interface avoids cold, stark tech conventions in favor of organic, ceramic-inspired surfaces, delicate typographic hierarchy, and gentle ambient depth.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from roasted coffee beans, steamed milk microfoam, unbleached linen, and garden herbs.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3D2B1F` - Espresso):** The anchor for high-contrast headlines, primary interactive elements, and focused typography. Deepened to `#2A1D15` for high-impact interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#58694E` - Olive Herb):** An earthy botanical tone reserved for accent badges, dietary tags, seasonal callouts, and secondary highlights. Paired with `#6B7F5E` for hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DFD5` - Oat Beige):** Used for structural separators, soft container fills, input boundaries, and card outlines.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Steamed Cream):** The base background environment, paired with a slightly richer tone (`#F7F3EB` - Warm Latte) for elevated containers, sheets, and grouped cards.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text renders in rich espresso (`#3D2B1F`) to maintain warm accessibility without the clinical harshness of true `#000000`. Muted supporting text utilizes `#7A6A5E`.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial grace with readable, approachable functionality.

### Hierarchy & Use
- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero narratives, category introductions, and section titles. Headlines capture the charm of printed menus and literary publications.
- **Functional Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a friendly, clear, geometric-humanist foundation for item descriptions, ingredient manifests, checkout processes, and administrative controls.
- **Microcopy & Labels:** Rendered in medium-to-semibold weights with slight tracking expansion to preserve legibility on small tags, prep times, and dietary labels.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture with deliberate breathing room ensures interactions feel calm, composed, and unhurried.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with generous outer margins (`3rem`) and broad column gutters (`1.5rem`). Max-width containers cap at `1200px` for menu browsing and `880px` for editorial tasting notes and story pages.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical page rhythm uses multiples of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) to `4rem` (`space-2xl`), giving full-bleed photography and menu typography space to settle naturally.
- In-component padding remains airy: cards use internal padding of `space-lg` to evoke tactile stationery cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on tonal layering, delicate linen-like surface shifts, and warm, diffused ambient shadows. Cold grays and heavy dark drops are strictly avoided.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Base Canvas:** `#FDFBF7` (Steamed Cream).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Lists):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F3EB` (Warm Latte), gently offset from the background.
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with warm rim highlights.

### Ambient Shadow Characteristics
- **Subtle Surface Elevation:** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.04), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals:** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Surfaces utilize a 1px border rendered in `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.07)` to retain clean boundary definition under soft natural light.

## Shapes

The interface embraces inviting, organic curves that echo smooth ceramic cups, pebble coasters, and warm baked goods.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem`), softening large structural blocks.
- **Buttons, Badges, and Chips:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) for high-touch, tactile delight.
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors:** Styled with `rounded-xl` (`1rem`) to maintain functional alignment while preserving warmth.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Espresso background (`#3D2B1F`), crisp cream text (`#FDFBF7`), pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover transitions smoothly to roasted black (`#2A1D15`) with a subtle `translate-y(-1px)` and ambient espresso glow.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm oat surface (`#F7F3EB`) with espresso text (`#3D2B1F`) and subtle 1px border (`#E8DFD5`). Hover state darkens gently to `#EFE7DC`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Unfilled, espresso text, underlined with 1px baseline offset on hover, ideal for subtle menu navigation.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- Dietary & origin tags (e.g., "Single Origin", "Oat Milk Alternative") use earthy olive (`#58694E` text on a soft `rgba(88, 105, 78, 0.12)` background).
- Roast level badges use soft oat beige (`#E8DFD5` fill with `#3D2B1F` text).

### Cards (Menu Items & Stories)
- **Menu Card:** Clean white or warm latte container (`#F7F3EB`), `rounded-2xl` corners, 1px border (`rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.06)`), and generous padding (`1.5rem`).
- **Product Imagery:** Contained within soft, radiused image wells (`rounded-xl`), framed with a warm tone overlay to harmonize photography with page hues.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Cream surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1rem` corner radius, 1px perimeter in `#E8DFD5`, with espresso label text positioned above. On active focus, transitions to a 1.5px espresso border (`#3D2B1F`) with zero harsh neon rings.
- **Selection Controls (Radios & Checkboxes):** Rounded-full (radios) and rounded-md (checkboxes) rendered in espresso `#3D2B1F` when checked, with a gentle cream checkmark.

### Lists & Menu Tiers
- Dot-leader or spacious alternating item rows separated by delicate warm beige dividers (`#E8DFD5`).
- Price points set in semibold `Plus Jakarta Sans` aligned gracefully opposite the item title and editorial flavor notes.

### Specialized Component: Tasting Note Radar / Flavor Tags
- Compact oval chips arranged inline, displaying flavor profiles (e.g., "Bergamot", "Brown Sugar", "Candied Pecan") using muted warm backgrounds with espresso borders.
